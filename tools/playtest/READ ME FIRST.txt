FAST TALKERS 0.9 - PLAYTEST - READ ME FIRST
============================================

You both need: a Windows PC, Steam, a headset, and to be Steam friends.
You both must use THIS SAME zip.


SETUP (both of you, 2 minutes)
------------------------------
1. Open Steam and log in. Leave Steam running.
2. Right-click the zip > "Extract All..." > Extract. (Don't run the game from inside the zip.)
3. Open the extracted "Fast Talkers" folder and double-click "Fast Talkers.exe".
   - Blue "Windows protected your PC" box?  Click "More info", then "Run anyway".
   - Windows Firewall asks about network access?  Tick both boxes and click "Allow".
   - Steam will say you are playing "Spacewar". That is normal - it is how the free
     Steam multiplayer trick works.


CONNECT (the host is whoever sets up the game)
----------------------------------------------
HOST:
  1. Title screen > PLAY ONLINE.
  2. Click HOST ON STEAM (INVITE FRIENDS).
  3. The Steam overlay opens. Invite your friend.
     (Closed it by accident? Click INVITE STEAM FRIENDS in the lobby.)

FRIEND:
  1. Start Fast Talkers FIRST and go to PLAY ONLINE. Wait on that screen.
  2. Accept the invite in Steam (the chat pop-up, or Shift+Tab > Friends).
     No invite showing? In the Steam friends list, right-click the host > "Join Game".

BOTH:
  - In the lobby, each pick a crew. Pick DIFFERENT crews so you can deal with each other.
  - The host clicks START THE WEEK.
    (Tick "Quick Week" first for a shorter game: Wednesday to Friday.)


TALKING
-------
- Your mic is always on. Players NEAR you hear you. Walls block people outside.
- Hold Y = crew radio (only your crew hears you, anywhere on the map).
- P = use the crew phone (it sits at your HQ). It calls the other crew's phone.


KEYS YOU NEED
-------------
WASD move, Shift sprint, Space jump (handbrake in a car), mouse aim, left click shoot,
R reload, 1-5 weapons, E use / enter car, M map, ENTER text chat,
B buy menu (morning only), G drop the case, K drop the phone,
X hands up, hold Z binoculars, F3 shows FPS, ESC menu / settings.


THE GAME IN 20 SECONDS
----------------------
A game is one WEEK. Each DAY (Monday to Friday) is one round.
You win on money BANKED at your HQ - not kills.
Make deals, trade the case, bluff, betray. Friday is the Dead Drop: no rules.


SOMETHING WRONG?
----------------
- No "HOST ON STEAM" button, or "Steam not running":
  start Steam, log in, then restart the game.
- "Different version of the game": one of you has an old copy. Both re-download this zip.
- Controller stopped working: Steam > Library > Spacewar > right-click > Properties >
  Controller > "Disable Steam Input". Restart the game.
- Joining just starts "Spacewar" instead: close it, start Fast Talkers first, and
  accept the invite again.
- Too slow? ESC > Settings > POTATO MODE.

More help, the full rules and a no-Steam fallback: "HOW TO PLAY WITH FRIENDS.txt".
Found a bug? Note the Day, what you were doing and where, and tell the host.
