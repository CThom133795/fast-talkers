FAST TALKERS - PLAYTEST BUILD - HOW TO PLAY WITH FRIENDS
=========================================================
Everyone must run the SAME zip. If one person has a different build, the join is refused
with "different version of the game".

STEAM (the easy way)
--------------------
1. Install Steam, start it and log in. Leave it running.
2. Unzip this folder anywhere (the Desktop is fine) and run "Fast Talkers.exe".
   Steam shows you as playing "Spacewar". That is expected: it is Valve's free test app,
   and it is how the invite system works before the game has its own Steam page.
   If Windows SmartScreen appears, click "More info", then "Run anyway".
3. HOST: PLAY ONLINE, then HOST ON STEAM (INVITE FRIENDS). The Steam overlay opens;
   pick your friends. (INVITE STEAM FRIENDS in the lobby opens it again.)
4. FRIENDS: START FAST TALKERS FIRST and stay on the title screen, then accept the Steam
   invite (the chat popup, or Shift+Tab > Friends). You can also right-click the host in your
   Steam friends list and pick "Join Game".
   Don't accept the invite while the game is closed: Steam would try to start Spacewar
   instead of Fast Talkers.
5. In the lobby everyone picks a crew. The host can tick "Quick Week" (Wednesday to Friday)
   for a shorter session, then presses START MATCH.
6. Use a headset. Voice is open mic: people near you hear you, and walls block passers-by.
   A name tag reading (( NAME )) means that player is talking to you.
   Hold Y for the crew radio: only your crew hears you, at any distance.
   N is push-to-talk if you turn open mic off in Settings.

Waiting for friends? SOLO WEEK on the title screen plays the same Week alone.

IF THE HOST ON STEAM BUTTON IS MISSING
--------------------------------------
The menu shows a line saying why (for example "Steam not running"). Start Steam, log in and
restart the game. Otherwise use the fallback below.

FALLBACK: DIRECT IP (ENet)
--------------------------
- Easiest over the internet: everyone installs Tailscale (free, tailscale.com/download) and
  joins the host's tailnet. The HOST presses HOST GAME; the lobby lists an address starting
  with 100. FRIENDS type it and press JOIN.
- Same house or Wi-Fi: use the host's 192.168.x.x or 10.x.x.x address.
- Without Tailscale: the host forwards UDP port 24650 to their PC. Friends join the host's
  public IP address.
- When Windows asks whether Fast Talkers may use the network, click Allow
  (tick Private and Public).

THE GAME IN ONE MINUTE
----------------------
- A match is a WEEK of five DAYS, Monday to Friday. Crews win on BANKED MONEY, not kills.
- Each morning you are at your HQ: a 30 s buy phase (B, or D-pad left) and the morning paper
  (yesterday's results in chat). Then 10 minutes on the street.
- Contracts grow through the Week: Monday pays x0.6, Friday pays x1.6.
- Mon-Thu: one crew holds THE LEDGER and another holds the CASH CASE. Bank the Ledger as
  the buyer, or steal either one and bank it at your own HQ.
  Clean deal (both banked, no kills between the two crews): a bonus for both.
- FRIDAY IS THE DEAD DROP. Both cases appear on a street when the doors open, and the chat
  names the spot. Whoever banks them gets paid. No rules.
- ONE case at a time. While you carry one, only your SIDEARM works. G drops it.
- THE CREW PHONE sits at your HQ spawn. Whoever holds it calls other crews: ENTER opens chat,
  TAB picks "CALL <crew>". K drops it. A stolen phone calls as its owners.
- Hands up: X (pad: R3). It really holsters your gun. Binoculars: hold Z (pad: hold D-pad up).
- Die and you drop what you carry, and you are back at HQ 10 s later with a pistol.
  Survivors keep their gear into tomorrow.
- F3 shows FPS. In Settings, POTATO MODE helps slow PCs.

Found a bug? Note the Day, what you were doing and roughly where, and tell the host.
